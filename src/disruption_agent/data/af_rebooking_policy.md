# Air France — Involuntary Rebooking Policy (Disruption Handling)

## Eligibility
A passenger is eligible for involuntary rebooking when the operating flight is
cancelled, delayed by more than 2 hours at departure, or when a missed connection
is caused by the carrier.

## Rebooking rules
- Rebooking on the next available Air France or KLM flight on the same
  origin-destination pair is free of charge, in the same or higher cabin, regardless of
  the original fare class or fare restrictions.
- Rebooking on codeshare and interline partners is permitted when no Air France or
  KLM alternative departs within 4 hours of the original scheduled time.
- For domestic French routes, rebooking onto TGV AIR rail services is permitted as
  re-routing at the earliest opportunity.

## Fare rules override
During an involuntary disruption, the following fare restrictions are waived:
non-refundable conditions, change fees, fare-difference collection, and minimum-stay
requirements.

## Refunds
If the passenger declines all rebooking options, the unused ticket coupons are
refundable to the original form of payment within 7 business days, including for
Light/Basic fares that are normally non-refundable.
