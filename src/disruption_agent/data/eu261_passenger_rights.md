# EU Regulation 261/2004 — Passenger Rights on Delay and Cancellation

## Scope
Applies to all flights departing from an EU airport, and to flights arriving in the EU
operated by an EU carrier.

## Right to care (delays)
- Delay of 2 hours or more (flights up to 1,500 km): meals and refreshments, two
  free communications (calls or emails).
- Delay of 5 hours or more: passengers may choose reimbursement of the full ticket
  price within 7 days plus, where relevant, a return flight to the first point of departure.
- Overnight delay: hotel accommodation and transport between airport and hotel
  must be provided by the operating carrier.

## Compensation (delays at final destination)
- 3 hours or more, flights up to 1,500 km: EUR 250.
- 3 hours or more, intra-EU flights over 1,500 km: EUR 400.
- 4 hours or more, all other flights over 3,500 km: EUR 600.
- No compensation is due when the disruption is caused by extraordinary
  circumstances (e.g. severe weather, air-traffic-control strikes) that could not have
  been avoided even if all reasonable measures had been taken.

## Cancellation
- Passengers must be offered the choice between reimbursement, re-routing at the
  earliest opportunity, or re-routing at a later date at the passenger's convenience.
- Compensation as per the delay bands above is due unless the passenger was
  informed at least 14 days before departure, or was offered re-routing arriving
  within limits close to the original schedule.
