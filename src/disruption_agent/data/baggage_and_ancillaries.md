# Baggage and Ancillary Services During Disruptions

## Checked baggage on rebooked flights
- Checked baggage allowance from the original ticket carries over to the new flight
  at no extra cost, including when the passenger is rebooked onto a partner carrier.
- If baggage was already checked in, it is transferred automatically; passengers
  should reclaim baggage only when the rebooked departure is on the next calendar day.

## Seat selection and ancillaries
- Paid seat selection is transferred when an equivalent seat is available; otherwise
  the seat fee is refunded proactively.
- Pre-paid lounge access remains valid for the rebooked departure.

## Special assistance
- Passengers with reduced mobility, unaccompanied minors, and passengers travelling
  with infants are rebooked with priority and must be offered assistance during the
  waiting period.
